Retail Sales Dashboard — January to February Project Overview Developed an interactive Retail Sales Dashboard in Power BI to analyze sales performance from January to February and transform raw transaction data into clear, actionable business insights. The dashboard was designed to give stakeholders a single view of overall sales performance, product and store performance, customer purchasing behavior, and operational trends. What I Did * Prepared and analyzed the retail sales dataset for reporting. * Created key KPI measures including Total Sales, Total Transactions, and Average Basket Size. * Built interactive slicers to allow stakeholders to filter and explore the dashboard dynamically. * Analyzed sales trends across January and February. * Created Sum of Total Price by Product to identify products contributing most to revenue. * Created Sum of Total Price by Store Region to compare performance across different locations. * Analyzed sales by product category to identify stronger and weaker-performing categories. * Evaluated cashier performance based on sales generated. * Analyzed payment methods to understand customer payment preferences. * Examined sales by day and hour to identify peak purchasing periods. * Used interactive visualizations to make the report easier for stakeholders to explore and interpret. * Added a narrative summary to communicate the main purpose and business value of the dashboard. Business Value The dashboard solves the problem of having to manually review large volumes of retail transaction data to understand business performance. It enables stakeholders to quickly identify revenue-driving products and regions, monitor sales activity, understand customer behavior, and identify patterns that can support inventory planning, staffing, marketing, and operational decisions. Overall, the project demonstrates my ability to use Power BI, data analysis, data visualization, KPI development, interactive reporting, and business storytelling to transform raw data into a practical decision-support tool. .

Revenue and Sales Insights Dashboard - Power BI

Revenue and Sales Insights Dashboard – January to March 2025/2026

Tool: Power BI


I developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard in Power BI to analyse sales data across January to March for 2025 and 2026. The project focused on transforming raw sales data into an interactive reporting solution that makes it easier to monitor performance, identify trends, and compare business results.

I prepared the data model by creating a dedicated Date table using DAX and establishing the necessary relationships between tables. I also developed advanced DAX measures for key performance calculations and created hierarchies to support more detailed analysis.

To improve user interaction, I implemented slicers, drill-down, drill-through, and a dedicated tooltip page, allowing users to move from high-level performance summaries into more detailed views of the data.

The dashboard was professionally formatted with consistent colours, KPI cards, visual hierarchy, navigation, and interactive elements, creating a clean and user-friendly reporting experience.

NHS Executive_Healthcare_Dashboard

Healthcare Analytics Dashboard | Built an interactive five-page healthcare analytics dashboard using appointment, payment, doctor, diagnosis and hospital data. The project analysed 100,000 appointments across 50 hospitals, 225 doctors and 2,000 patients, with dedicated analysis for hospital operations, patient demographics, clinical diagnoses and financial performance. Developed reusable DAX measures using SUM, COUNT, DISTINCTCOUNT, AVERAGE, CALCULATE, TOPN, SUMMARIZE, MAXX and SELECTEDVALUE. Created executive KPI cards, Top 10 hospital and doctor rankings, patient demographic charts, diagnosis analysis, payment-method and payment-status analysis, insurance coverage analysis and monthly revenue trends. Implemented interactive Hospital, Gender, Age Group and Appointment Status slicers, synchronized slicers across pages, and dynamic KPI titles using DAX and Power BI's field-value title functionality. The project also involved troubleshooting noisy date visualisations, incorrect chart types, duplicate counting of doctors, and dashboard usability issues. Tools: Microsoft Power BI, DAX, relational healthcare data Key areas: Healthcare Analytics • Business Intelligence • Data Visualisation • DAX • KPI Development • Interactive Dashboards • Operational Analytics • Financial Analytics • Patient Analytics
